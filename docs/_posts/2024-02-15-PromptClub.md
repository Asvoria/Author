Prompt Club is a satirical lit-fic with sprinkles of sci-fi, heavily on modern social commentary about machine learning, probability math, and human evolution. It's also a cynical, yet dellusional, first-person narrative that skewers the modern publishing industry, commodification of art, and the superficiality of chasing market trends.
